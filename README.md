# 📱 Electronics Sales Performance & Revenue Leakage Analysis | Power BI Dashboard

## Short Description
A 3-page Power BI dashboard analyzing **10K orders** and **$48M in revenue** across an electronics retail business — covering sales performance, customer segmentation by age and loyalty, and a dedicated revenue leakage analysis tracking **$16.07M in cancellation losses**.

## 📂 Data Source
- **Platform:** [Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/)
- **Scope:** Electronics retail dataset (2023–2024) covering orders, revenue, product types, payment methods, shipping, and customer demographics

---

## ✨ Key Findings & Highlights

### 📊 Page 1 — Sales Overview
- Total Revenue: **$48M** | Orders: **10K** | Avg Order Value: **$4.69K** | Units Sold: **83K**
- **Smartphones lead all products at $16.2M**, nearly **1.5x the next category** (Smartwatch at $10.8M)
- **Credit Card and PayPal together drive $28M (58% of revenue)** — digital payments dominate
- **Standard shipping accounts for $16.4M** — more than Expedited and Same Day combined
- Revenue grew steadily from **$0.4M (Sep 2023) to $5.1M (Aug 2024)** — strong business growth trend

### 👥 Page 2 — Customer Segmentation
- Loyalty Revenue: **$10.38M (21.53%)** | Non-Loyalty Revenue: **$37.81M**
- **55+ age group is the top revenue segment at ~$19.8M** — older customers spend the most
- **Non-loyalty customers spend ~$900 more per order** ($4.4K vs $3.5K) across all age groups — loyalty program is not driving higher spend
- **18–24 segment is the smallest** at only ~$5.1M — a large untapped younger market

### 🚨 Page 3 — Revenue Leakage
- Cancelled Orders: **4,987** | Cancellation Rate: **48.57%** | Lost Revenue: **$16.07M**
- **Nearly 1 in 2 orders is being cancelled** — a critical operational problem
- **Smartphones alone account for $5.4M in lost revenue** from cancellations
- **Credit Card cancellations lost $4.92M** — the highest among all payment methods
- **Standard shipping lost $5.4M** — same-day and expedited also leaking $3.2M and $3.0M respectively

---

## 💡 Recommendations
- **Fix the 48.57% cancellation rate urgently** — recovering even 20% of lost orders brings back **~$3.2M in revenue**
- **Investigate smartphone cancellations** — $5.4M lost from a single product category needs immediate root cause analysis
- **Revamp the loyalty program** — loyalty members are spending less per order; the program needs better incentives to drive higher basket size
- **Target the 18–24 segment** — currently the weakest group; digital-first campaigns and flexible payment options could unlock significant growth
- **Review standard shipping experience** — $5.4M in cancellations tied to standard shipping suggests delivery time dissatisfaction
